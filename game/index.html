A card duel by the fire. Outwit the Cinder Warden,
spend your embers wisely, and burn bright.

How to play

  1. Each turn you gain ember mana and draw a card.
  2. Click a card to play it — if you can afford its cost.
  3. Strike cards damage the Warden. Guard cards block damage. Mend cards heal you.
  4. Reduce the Warden to 0 life to win. If your life hits 0, the fire goes out.

Keys: 1–5 play a card   E end turn   R restart
